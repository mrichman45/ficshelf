      Reading

      Your AO3 account

      Optional. Signing in lets you save works their authors have restricted to registered users. Without it, everything public still works.

      This is the username you log in with, which is not always the name shown on your works.

      Signed in as

      Your password is handed to your operating system's own password store — KDE Wallet on Linux, Credential Manager on Windows — and is never written to this app's database or settings. It is sent to AO3 and nowhere else. Sign out and forget deletes it.

      On a phone your password is not stored anywhere. It is sent to AO3 once to sign in, and you stay signed in until the app is closed. Next time, sign in again here.

      About fetching from AO3

      FicShelf downloads a work using AO3's own Download link, the same one you would click on the page, one at a time with a pause in between. It keeps nothing but your own copies on your own disk and serves nothing to anyone else. Works restricted to registered users need you to be signed in above.
