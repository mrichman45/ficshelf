      Tapping the page

      Signed in as

      Your password is handed to your operating system's own password store — KDE Wallet on Linux, Credential Manager on Windows — and is never written to this app's database or settings. It is sent to AO3 and nowhere else. Sign out and forget deletes it.

      On a phone your password is not stored anywhere. It is sent to AO3 once to sign in, and you stay signed in until the app is closed. Next time, sign in again here.

      About fetching from AO3

      FicShelf downloads a work using AO3's own Download link, the same one you would click on the page, one at a time with a pause in between. It keeps nothing but your own copies on your own disk and serves nothing to anyone else. Works restricted to registered users need you to be signed in above. If AO3 ever says to slow down or that it is overloaded, FicShelf stops asking for a while (5 minutes, longer if it happens again) and shows how long on the AO3 screens.

      Backup and restore

      A backup is one file with your library list, reading positions, bookmarks, highlights, notes, edited details and settings. Everything also packs in the books, AO3 downloads and podfics kept inside FicShelf, so it can be large. Books you added from elsewhere on the device are not copied.

      On this phone, backups are saved to Download/FicShelf. Copy the file somewhere safe (a computer, Google Drive) to keep it if the phone is lost or reset.

      Error log

      Every error FicShelf shows is also written here, newest first, with what it was doing at the time. It stays on this device. Use Copy log to paste it somewhere when something goes wrong.
